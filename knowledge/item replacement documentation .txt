# Items Replacement — Business Documentation
 
## 1. Executive Summary
 
The **Items Replacement** feature handles orders where a merchant cannot fulfill one or more items after the customer places the order. Instead of rejecting the whole order immediately, the platform can:
 
- Notify the customer and offer **pre-configured alternative items**
 
- Let the customer **replace unavailable items** and recalculate the order total
 
- Apply the customer's **unavailable-items preference** (suggest alternatives, remove and continue, or cancel order)
 
- Support a **different-quantity** scenario (same item, lower quantity)
 
- Remove an entire merchant from a **multi-merchant order** when the customer declines all replacements
 
The feature is controlled by the Esquio feature flag `ItemReplacement` and spans the **Restaurant Portal**, **Customer API**, **notifications**, **robocalls**, and **order/payment recalculation** logic.
 
---
 
## 2. Business Problem & Goals
 
| Problem | Solution |
 
|--------|----------|
 
| Merchant marks items unavailable after order placement | Structured availability workflow per line item |
 
| Customer loses the whole order when one item is missing | Per-item handling with alternatives or partial continuation |
 
| No standard way to suggest substitutes | Merchant configures replacement items on menu items |
 
| Price changes after substitution | Preview and commit replacement with full order recalculation |
 
| Different customers want different behavior | Customer-level preference stored on profile |
 
**Business goals:**
 
- Reduce full-order rejections due to stock issues
 
- Keep customers informed and in control
 
- Preserve order value where possible
 
- Maintain accurate totals, discounts, delivery fees, and wallet/payment balances

| Control | Value |
 
|---------|-------|
 
| Feature flag | `ItemReplacement` (Esquio) |
 
| Restaurant UI | Shown only when flag is enabled |
 
| Customer API | `GetUnavailableItems` and `CalculateItemsReplacementTotal` are gated by `[FeatureFilter(Name = ItemReplacement)]` |

---
 
## 5. Customer Unavailable-Items Preference
 
Stored on `Customer.UnavailableItemsPreference`:
 
| Preference | Enum Value | Business Meaning |
 
|-------------|------------|------------------|
 
| **Suggest Alternatives** | `0` (default) | Customer should be offered replacements when merchant marks items unavailable with alternatives |
 
| **Remove and Continue** | `1` | Unavailable items are removed automatically; order continues with remaining items |
 
| **Cancel Order** | `2` | Order should be rejected when merchant cannot fulfill unavailable items |
 
**Customer API:** `POST api/CustomerUser/UpdateUnavailableItemsPreference`
 
**Restaurant UI:** Preference is displayed on the order details screen when the feature is enabled.

## 6. Core Concepts & Terminology
 
| Term | Meaning |
 
|------|---------|
 
| **Menu Item Replacement** | Catalog-level mapping: original item → up to 3 substitute items (`TA_MenuItemReplacements`) |
 
| **Order Detail Replacement** | Order-level suggestion saved when merchant notifies customer (`TA_OrderDetailReplacements`), including optional quantity |
 
| **Has Alternatives** | Line item flagged as unavailable but with merchant-suggested replacements |
 
| **Not Available Items** | Line item marked unavailable by merchant |
 
| **Is Replacement** | New line item added after customer confirms a substitute |
 
| **Suggest Alternatives** | Merchant availability option: different item(s) as replacement |
 
| **Different Quantity** | Same item offered at a lower quantity (self-replacement) |
 
| **Items Under Replacement** | Restaurant order status while waiting for customer action |

## 8. Merchant Workflow (Restaurant Portal)
 
**Screen:** `New-OrderDetails` (`TalabatkRestaurants`)
 
### 8.1 Preconditions
 
- Feature flag `ItemReplacement` enabled
 
- Order status is `Pending` or `RestaurantPending`
 
- Order not already confirmed or rejected
 
- Customer preference shown to merchant
 
### 8.2 Per-Item Availability Options
 
When feature is enabled and customer preference is **Suggest Alternatives**:
 
| Option | Effect |
 
|--------|--------|
 
| **Available** | Item remains in order |
 
| **Not Available** | Item unavailable; no alternatives |
 
| **Suggest Alternatives** | Item unavailable; merchant must configure replacement item(s) |
 
| **Different Quantity** | Same item as replacement with lower quantity; no "not available time" required |
 
### 8.3 Configuring Alternatives
 
1. Merchant clicks **Alternatives** (only when `hasAlternatives = true` and customer preference = Suggest Alternatives).
 
2. **Alternatives popup** opens with menu tree/search.
 
3. Merchant selects up to **3 replacement items**.
 
4. For **same-item replacement**, quantity defaults to `ordered quantity - 1` and must be `>= 1` and `< ordered quantity`.
 
5. On confirm:
 
   - Replacement IDs are saved to the **menu item catalog** (future orders).
 
   - Quantities are kept in local UI state until **Notify Customer** is clicked.
 
### 8.4 Notify Customer
 
Triggered when:
 
- Some items are unavailable or have alternatives, and
 
- Restaurant status is before `Confirmed`
 
**Validations before notify:**
 
- Replacement cannot happen twice on same order (`isReplacement` check)
 
- Items marked with alternatives must have at least one replacement configured
 
- Availability time required for unavailable/alternative items (except Different Quantity)
 
- Cooking time required when removing items under Remove and Continue
 
**API:** `POST api/Orders/NotifyCustomerToReplaceItems`
 
**Payload per line item:**
 
- `available`, `hasAlternatives`, `availabilityId`, `rejectionReasonId`
 
- `itemReplacements[]` with `itemId` and optional `quantity`
 
### 8.5 Post-Notify Merchant Experience
 
- Grid becomes read-only (`ItemsUnderReplacement` status)
 
- Merchant waits for customer action
 
- After customer updates order, merchant receives push + real-time update

## 9. Customer Workflow (Mobile App / Customer API)
 
### 9.1 Get Unavailable Items
 
**API:** `GET api/Order/GetUnavailableItems?orderId=&restaurantId=`
 
Returns unavailable line items for the authenticated customer, including:
 
- Original item name, image, price
 
- Whether alternatives exist
 
- Suggested replacement items from `TA_MenuItemReplacements`, filtered to active/available items
 
- Offer discount messaging per replacement
 
- `MaxQuantity` when merchant suggested a self-replacement quantity
 
- `QuantityDifferenceText` for different-quantity cases
 
### 9.2 Preview / Commit Replacement
 
**API:** `POST api/Order/CalculateItemsReplacementTotal`
 
| Parameter | Purpose |
 
|-----------|---------|
 
| `CommitUpdate = false` | Preview new total and messages only |
 
| `CommitUpdate = true` | Persist order changes |
 
| `ReplacedDetails[]` | Per unavailable line: selected replacement as full cart item (item, price, options, quantity) |
 
**Response (`ItemReplacementResult`):**
 
- `HeaderMessage` — same/less/more money scenario
 
- `Message` — cash/wallet adjustment explanation
 
- `Details[]` — old vs new item comparison per line
 
### 9.3 Customer Declines All Replacements (Multi-Merchant Only)
 
If **all** unavailable items from one merchant have **no replacement selected** and the order has **more than one merchant**:
 
- That merchant's items are removed from the order
 
- Order total is recalculated
 
- Merchant is notified of customer update
 
If it is a **single-merchant order**, customer must select at least one replacement (`SelectOneReplacement` error).
 
### 9.4 Remove Merchant Explicitly
 
**API:** `POST api/Order/DeleteRestaurant?orderId=&restaurantId=`
 
Separate endpoint for customer-initiated merchant removal (also used in broader order-update flows).

## 10. Business Rules by Customer Preference
 
### 10.1 Suggest Alternatives (`0`)
 
| Merchant Action | System Behavior |
 
|----------------|-----------------|
 
| Some unavailable + alternatives configured | Notify customer, set status to Items Under Replacement |
 
| All unavailable, none with alternatives | **Reject order** (if no self-replacement items) |
 
| Customer selects replacements | Replace lines, recalculate, return merchant to Pending |
 
| Customer ignores | Repeated push notifications (configurable) + robocall |
 
### 10.2 Remove and Continue (`1`)
 
| Condition | System Behavior |
 
|-----------|-----------------|
 
| Unavailable items, **no** self-replacement (same item) | Remove unavailable items, recalculate, confirm remaining items if cooking time provided |
 
| Single-merchant order, **all** items unavailable | Reject entire order |
 
| Multi-merchant, partial unavailable | Remove only unavailable lines for that merchant |
 
Customer receives localized **"Some items were removed"** notification.
 
### 10.3 Cancel Order (`2`)
 
| Condition | System Behavior |
 
|-----------|-----------------|
 
| Any unavailable items without viable alternatives | Reject restaurant portion; reject whole order if single merchant or all merchants rejected |
 
| Merchant UI warning | If some items still available, merchant is informed that preference is cancel |
 
### 10.4 Self-Replacement (Same Item) Special Rules
 
- Counted when merchant includes the **same menu item** in replacement list
 
- Item is **not** auto-marked unavailable in catalog when different quantity is used
 
- Menu item stays **available** if configured with self-replacement
 
- UI treats this as **Different Quantity**, not Suggest Alternatives

## 11. Order & Status Lifecycle
 
### 11.1 Order-Level Status
 
| Status | When |
 
|--------|------|
 
| `RestaurantNotAvilableItems (14)` | After merchant notifies customer about unavailable items |
 
### 11.2 Restaurant-Level Status
 
| Status | Code | When |
 
|--------|------|------|
 
| `ItemAvailability` | 140 | Items marked unavailable/available during review |
 
| `ItemsUnderReplacement` | 200 | Customer notified and awaiting replacement decision |
 
| `Pending` | 110 | After customer commits replacement (merchant must re-confirm) |
 
| `Confirmed` | 130 | Merchant confirms after replacement or available items |
 
### 11.3 Line-Item Flags
 
| Field | Meaning |
 
|-------|---------|
 
| `NotAvilableItems` | Merchant marked item unavailable |
 
| `HasAlternatives` | Merchant offered replacements |
 
| `IsReplacement` | Line added by customer as substitute |
 
| `IsAvailable` | Merchant marked item as available |
 
### 11.4 Replacement Allowed Once
 
If any line in the order already has `IsReplacement = true`, merchant cannot trigger notify again. UI shows **"Replacement only once"**.

## 12. Data Model
 
### 12.1 `TA_MenuItemReplacements` (Catalog)
 
| Column | Description |
 
|--------|-------------|
 
| `OriginalItemId` | Item being replaced |
 
| `ReplacementItemId` | Suggested substitute |
 
- Composite PK `(OriginalItemId, ReplacementItemId)`
 
- Max **3** replacements per item (validated in `EditMenuItemValidator`)
 
- Managed via menu item add/edit in Restaurant Portal
 
### 12.2 `TA_OrderDetailReplacements` (Per Order)
 
| Column | Description |
 
|--------|-------------|
 
| `OrderDetailId` | Unavailable line |
 
| `ReplacementItemId` | Suggested item |
 
| `Quantity` | Suggested quantity (especially for different-quantity cases) |
 
- Written when merchant calls `NotifyCustomerToReplaceItems`
 
- Existing replacements for a line are replaced on re-notify
 
### 12.3 Related Entities
 
- `Customer.UnavailableItemsPreference`
 
- `OrderDetails.NotAvilableItems`, `HasAlternatives`, `IsReplacement`
 
- `OrderRestaurantDetails.RestaurantOrderStatus`
 
- `RoboCalls` with type `CustomerItemReplacement`

## 14. Notifications & Robocalls
 
### 14.1 Push Notifications
 
**Job:** `NotifyCustomerForOrderActionJob` (Hangfire, `restaurant` queue)
 
| Trigger | Message |
 
|---------|---------|
 
| Alternatives offered | Localized `ReplaceItems` |
 
| No alternatives / cannot proceed | `RestaurantCannotProceedOrder` |
 
| Remove and continue | `SomeItemsWereRemoved` |
 
**Notification metadata:**
 
- `entity_type = ReplaceItems (19)`
 
- `entity_id = orderId`
 
- Custom sound: `take_action`
 
**Retry behavior:**
 
- `ReplaceItemsNotificationIntervalInMinutes` — interval between reminders
 
- `MaxRunsForReplaceItemsNotification` — max reminder attempts
 
- Reminders suppressed if unavailable items already resolved (for Suggest Alternatives)
 
### 14.2 Robocall
 
Triggered in `NotifyCustomerToReplaceItems` when `Configuration.MaximumCustomerRobocall > 0`.
 
| Setting | Purpose |
 
|---------|---------|
 
| `ZiwoSettings.ItemReplacementStorageName` | Audio file for replacement prompt |
 
| `RoboCallType.CustomerItemReplacement` | Call classification |
 
Follow-up robocalls can be scheduled via `RobocallScheduler.HandleItemReplacementCall` if customer still has unavailable items.
 
### 14.3 Merchant Notification
 
After customer commits replacement:
 
- Push: "The order has been updated by the customer."
 
- Real-time order update to restaurant via `SendOrderUpdateForRestaurant`

## 15. Financial & Pricing Impact
 
When replacements are committed, the system recalculates:
 
| Area | Behavior |
 
|------|----------|
 
| Line items | Unavailable lines removed; new replacement lines added |
 
| Promo codes | Re-evaluated against updated order |
 
| Vouchers | Re-evaluated if voucher campaign enabled |
 
| Tiered discounts | Recalculated per affected merchant |
 
| Offers | Active offers re-applied; replacement offer items included |
 
| Delivery fees | Recalculated from remaining merchants/zones |
 
| Service fees | City min/max/percentage rules applied |
 
| Wallet | Balance applied; transaction descriptions in EN/AR |
 
| Online payment | If captured and new total lower → wallet refund message; if higher → additional cash message |
 
**Customer-facing total messages:**
 
| Scenario | Header | Footer |
 
|----------|--------|--------|
 
| Same total | Items replaced with same money | Same total after update |
 
| Lower total | Items replaced with less money | Cash deduction or wallet refund |
 
| Higher total | Items replaced with more money | Additional cash required |
 
**Validations on replacement items:**
 
- Item, price, and options must be active/available
 
- Option min/max constraints enforced
 
- Max quantity per day/order enforced per price tier

## 16. Menu Item Configuration (Merchant Catalog)
 
Configured in:
 
- **Order flow:** Alternatives popup (saves to menu item on confirm)
 
- **Menu management:** `category-item-form-popup` (`replacementIds` field)
 
**Rules:**
 
- Max 3 replacement item IDs
 
- Replacements stored on `MenuItem.ItemReplacements`
 
- If item includes itself as replacement → item remains available (supports different-quantity scenario)
 
- For **Mart stores**, search/tree uses store-specific product lookup

## 17. Mart vs Restaurant Differences
 
| Aspect | Restaurant | Mart (Store) |
 
|--------|------------|--------------|
 
| Unavailable item in catalog | Temporary unavailability window | Item **deactivated** |
 
| Replacement picker | Menu tree + search | Store product search with lookup data |
 
| Delivery fee cap | City max tax | `MaximumDeliveryFeesForStore` |
 
| Self-replacement availability | Item not marked unavailable | Item not deactivated |

## 18. Multi-Merchant Order Behavior
 
| Scenario | Outcome |
 
|----------|---------|
 
| Merchant A has unavailable items, Merchant B fine | Only Merchant A enters replacement flow |
 
| Customer replaces items for A | Order continues; A returns to Pending |
 
| Customer selects no replacements for A in multi-order | Merchant A removed; order recalculated with B |
 
| Single-merchant, all unavailable, no replacements | Error — must select at least one replacement |
 
| All merchants eventually confirmed | Order auto-confirms when all restaurant details are Confirmed |

## 20. Validations & Guardrails
 
### Merchant-Side
 
- Order must be Pending / RestaurantPending
 
- Cannot update confirmed or rejected orders
 
- Alternatives required when `hasAlternatives = true`
 
- Availability time required except Different Quantity
 
- Cooking time required when continuing with partial availability
 
- Replacement only once per order
 
### Customer-Side
 
- Order must belong to authenticated customer
 
- Replacement items must be currently available
 
- Selected price must be available
 
- Options must satisfy category min/max rules
 
- Quantity limits per price tier respected
 
- Cannot update order with failed/pending online payment when removing merchant
 
### Catalog-Side
 
- Max 3 replacements per menu item
 
- Replacement items must exist in same restaurant catalog

## 23. Key Business Scenarios (Worked Examples)
 
### Scenario A — Happy path with alternatives
 
1. Customer preference: Suggest Alternatives
 
2. Merchant marks Item X as unavailable with alternatives Y, Z
 
3. Customer notified → selects Y
 
4. Total increases by 5 EGP → customer sees "more money" message
 
5. Merchant receives update → confirms order
 
### Scenario B — Remove and continue
 
1. Customer preference: Remove and Continue
 
2. Merchant marks Item X unavailable (no self-replacement)
 
3. System removes X, recalculates, confirms remaining items
 
4. Customer notified that items were removed
 
### Scenario C — Different quantity
 
1. Customer ordered 3× Item X
 
2. Merchant selects X as replacement with quantity 2
 
3. Customer sees quantity difference text
 
4. On confirm, same item replaced at lower quantity
 
### Scenario D — Multi-merchant decline
 
1. Order has Restaurant A + B
 
2. All A items unavailable; customer selects no replacements
 
3. Restaurant A removed; order continues with B only
 
### Scenario E — Cancel preference
 
1. Customer preference: Cancel Order
 
2. Merchant marks items unavailable without alternatives
 
3. Restaurant portion rejected; whole order rejected if single merchant

## 25. Glossary
 
| Term | Definition |
 
|------|------------|
 
| **Alternative** | A different menu item offered instead of an unavailable one |
 
| **Self-replacement** | Same item offered at a lower quantity |
 
| **Notify Customer** | Merchant action that triggers customer-facing workflow |
 
| **Commit Update** | Customer confirmation that persists replacement changes |
 
| **Preview** | Customer views new total without saving |
